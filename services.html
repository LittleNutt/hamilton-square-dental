Services

General, cosmetic, restorative, and periodontal care in one familiar office.

The services below are organized from Hamilton Square Dental's existing service menu and rewritten for a cleaner patient experience.

Treatment Menu

Find the care you are looking for.

Cleanings and Exams

Comprehensive exams, cleanings, polishing, and guidance for improving oral hygiene routines at home.

Preventive

Sealants

A protective coating for narrow grooves and pits that are difficult to clean thoroughly with a toothbrush.

Preventive

Bonding and White Fillings

Composite resin restorations used for decay, cracks, stains, or replacing the look of silver fillings when appropriate.

Cosmetic

Whitening

Tooth whitening options for patients who want a brighter smile, including in-office whitening noted on the existing site.

Cosmetic

Veneers

Thin porcelain restorations bonded to the front of teeth to address shape, color, chips, or cosmetic concerns.

Cosmetic

Crowns and Bridges

Full-coverage restorations and tooth replacement options that help restore appearance, function, and bite support.

Restorative

Full and Partial Dentures

Removable appliances for replacing some or all missing teeth, planned around each patient's needs.

Restorative

Root Canals

Endodontic treatment used when infection, trauma, deep decay, or cracks affect the nerve of a tooth.

Restorative

Crown Lengthening

A procedure that adjusts gum and bone levels so a broken or severely decayed tooth can be restored.

Periodontal

Scaling and Root Planing

Deep cleaning below the gum line to remove calculus and plaque and support soft-tissue reattachment.

Periodontal

Extractions

Tooth removal may be recommended for severe decay, fracture, infection, or other clinical reasons.

Restorative

Dental Implants

Implants can replace missing teeth or stabilize dentures, helping restore function and appearance.

Restorative
Technology

Tools that support diagnosis, comfort, and education.

The existing technology page lists digital X-rays, VELscope screening, DIAGNOdent, intraoral cameras, rotary endodontics, and laser dentistry. In the redesign, these belong alongside services as proof of a thoughtful clinical environment.
