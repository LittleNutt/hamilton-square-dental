Services

General, cosmetic, restorative, and periodontal care in one familiar office.

Treatment Directory

Organized care without the clutter.

01

Preventive Dentistry

Routine care designed to maintain dental health and catch issues early.

  • Cleanings and exams
  • Sealants
  • Home-care guidance
  • Nightguards when recommended
02

Cosmetic Dentistry

Options that support a brighter, more natural-looking smile while respecting function.

  • Bonding
  • White fillings
  • Whitening
  • Porcelain veneers
03

Restorative Dentistry

Care for broken, worn, missing, or infected teeth.

  • Crowns
  • Bridges
  • Full and partial dentures
  • Root canals
04

Implant Dentistry

Tooth replacement options for missing teeth and added stability for some denture patients.

  • Dental implants
  • Implant-supported restoration planning
  • Denture stabilization
05

Periodontal Care

Treatment focused on gum health and restorability.

  • Scaling and root planing
  • Crown lengthening
  • Soft-tissue support
06

Surgical Care

When a tooth cannot be predictably restored, removal may be recommended.

  • Extractions
  • Care planning after fracture, decay, or infection
