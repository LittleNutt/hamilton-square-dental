About The Practice

A private dental practice built around comfort, education, and lasting relationships.

Hamilton Square Dental presents itself as a place where patients are treated like family, understand their care, and receive modern general dentistry in a welcoming office setting.

Dr. Wellington S. Tsai

A scientific background with a patient-first chairside style.

Dr. Wellington S. Tsai graduated from the University of Michigan with a B.Sc. in Biology and a B.A. in Asian Studies. He continued with graduate studies in physiology at North Carolina State University and cellular molecular biology at the University of Michigan before earning his D.M.D. from UMDNJ.

He completed residency training at Jersey Shore University Medical Center. The existing practice biography also notes his research work, journal publications, guest lectures, and hospital role as an attending dentist and director of dental research at the JSUMC Dental Department.

  • Member of the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, New Jersey Dental Association, and Monmouth Ocean County Dental Society.
  • Experienced with complex dental conditions through a background that combines clinical dentistry, science, and teaching.
  • Known on the practice site for putting patient comfort first and helping patients understand their treatment.
